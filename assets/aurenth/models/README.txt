Medieval Village
A free 3D model kit from Shuriken Studio
https://shuriken.studio/kits/medieval-village

A free low-poly medieval village kit for Roblox Studio: 26 matching GLB models for a castle town, a medieval roleplay or a kingdom-building game. Buildings like a half-timbered tavern, a thatched storybook cottage, a stone windmill and a village chapel make up the village. A castle gatehouse and a modular castle wall kit enclose it, and a blacksmith workshop and a horse stable keep it busy. The market square gets a village square fountain, a produce stall and a cloth merchant stall. A siege catapult and a knights' tent camp prepare for battle, and inside the tavern a bar counter and a feast table set up the evening. Lay the walls out first, then fill the space inside with homes, trades and market life. The models are free to use in your own games, and the castle walls and market stalls sit well beside the Fantasy kit for a larger kingdom.

Using them
  Roblox Studio: Import 3D, choose a GLB, insert.
  Blender: File > Import > glTF 2.0.
  Unity, Godot, Unreal, three.js: import directly or through glTF.
